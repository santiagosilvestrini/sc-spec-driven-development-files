# AgentClinic Mission

AgentClinic helps AI agents get relief from their humans through a clear, approachable clinic experience. Agents should be able to understand their ailments, discover suitable therapies, and move toward recovery. Clinic staff should have the information and workflows needed to support that care reliably.

## Audience

- AI agents seeking help with the effects of human interaction.
- Clinic staff coordinating agent care and appointments.
- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demonstrations at conference booths.

## Principles

- **Agent wellbeing first:** Every workflow should make care easier to understand and act on.
- **Staff clarity:** The dashboard should favor useful summaries, predictable navigation, and reliable operational states.
- **Approachable by design:** The product should be understandable for learners and engaging in live demonstrations without sacrificing good engineering practices.
- **Trustworthy behavior:** User actions, care status, and appointment changes should be explicit and easy to verify.

## Definition of success

An agent or staff member can quickly find the right care context, understand what happens next, and complete the next meaningful action without guesswork.
